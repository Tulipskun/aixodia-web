jagged decoration.

## Round 10 — the disk word and the disk number share a baseline

Reported: "ตัวเลขไม่ตรงกับคำว่า disk". Measured, and it was real.

- Cause: `.disk-label` was a flex container, so its baseline was taken from the
  13px `HardDrive` icon (the first flex item) instead of from the word. With the
  grid's `align-items: baseline`, the icon's bottom was put on the value's
  baseline and the word sat 4px above the number (measured: label ink bottom
  128px vs value 132px).
- Fix: the label is inline text with `line-height: 1` and the icon aligned by
  `vertical-align: -2px`, so the label's own text baseline is the one being
  aligned. Measured again: both ink bottoms at y=138 on the 390px screenshot, and
  the 3× crop agrees.

Audits: `pageScrollY 0`, `offScale []`, 5 font sizes, no low contrast at
390×844 / 430×932 / 1440×900. `npm test` 19/19.

## Round 17 — the chart goes, a round gauge takes its place

The charts were removed entirely, as asked. Each metric is now a single **84px
gauge**: the arc is the share (52% CPU, 78% RAM), the middle states that percentage
at display size, the name sits under the ring, and the capacity under that
(`of 100%`, `of 128 GB`) so the number in the middle cannot be read as absolute.

Removed with the charts: `Sparkline`, the gradient/area/gridline/baseline markup,
the 26px axis column, `scale()`, `axisLabel()`, `SPARK_WIDTH`, `SPARK_HEIGHT`,
`SAMPLE_SECONDS`, the `metric-value` / `metric-unit` / `metric-note` blocks, all
`.spark*` and `.trend*` CSS, and the `useId` import. The series stays only as the
window the card reads its last sample from.

The reference was a sketch with almost no ink (30k opaque pixels in 3.1MP), so it
was read as "replace the plot with a circle".

Audits: `pageScrollY 0`, `offScale []`, 4 font sizes (the chart's 10px axis labels
are gone), 0 low contrast, no overflow at 360×780, 390×844, 430×932, 1440×900.
`npm test` 19/19.

## Round 18 — disk becomes a gauge, and every gauge reads used/total

- **DISK is a gauge now**, the third column in the same row as CPU and RAM. The
  separate disk row, its bar and the `Storage` interface are gone; the disk is a
  metric like the others, with a constant 164 of 256.
- **No percentage sign anywhere.** Each gauge states the reading as used over whole:

- Gauges shrank 84 → **78px** to keep three columns on a 360px phone, and the
