## Round 2 — storage to the header, charts redrawn

Requested: the storage bar was a full-width band and the charts still read as
jagged decoration.

- **Storage** is now a compact meter in the card's top-right, under the status
  pill: `disk · 164 / 256 GB` over a 4px, 124px bar. The full-width band and its
  `.storage` rules were deleted, not hidden.
- **Charts** are Catmull-Rom curves (gentle control offsets) instead of polylines,
  with a gradient fill, a faint zero baseline, an end marker with a halo, and a
  scale anchored at zero with 15% headroom — two charts at the same height now
  mean the same thing instead of each inventing its own range. Eight points per
  series at this width.

Audits after round 2: `pageScrollY 0`, `offScale []`, 5 font sizes, no low
contrast, no wrapped controls, no overlapping at 390×844 / 430×932 / 1440×900.

## Round 4 — 10 s samples over ten minutes, a range table, no end marker

- Every series is now 60 samples at one sample per 10 s, i.e. the last ten
  minutes, generated by a deterministic walk so the mock looks identical on each
  load. The chart states the window in a caption: `last 10 min · 1 sample / 10 s`.
- The line is a plain polyline (`M … L …`) through all 60 samples — no smoothing,
  no end marker, no halo. Stroke 1.2px so density reads as texture.
- A `min / avg / max` table sits under the charts. Cells carry the metric's own
  unit (`4.4 GB`), never the capacity, which stays in the headline reading.
- The headline reading is now number + quiet unit (`5.4` + small `/ 8 GB`), which
  is what let it fit a third of a 390px screen without an ellipsis.
- `Machine.version` was already gone; `Metric.value` also went, because the
  reading is derived from the last sample instead of typed twice.

Audits: `pageScrollY 0`, `offScale []`, 5 font sizes, no low contrast, no overflow
at 390×844 / 430×932 / 1440×900. `npm test` 19/19, build clean.
