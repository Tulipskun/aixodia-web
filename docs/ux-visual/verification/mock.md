## Round 10 — the disk word and the disk number share a baseline

Reported: "ตัวเลขไม่ตรงกับคำว่า disk". Measured, and it was real.

- Cause: `.disk-label` was a flex container, so its baseline was taken from the
  13px `HardDrive` icon (the first flex item) instead of from the word. With the
  grid's `align-items: baseline`, the icon's bottom was put on the value's

- Fix: the label is inline text with `line-height: 1` and the icon aligned by
  `vertical-align: -2px`, so the label's own text baseline is the one being
  aligned. Measured again: both ink bottoms at y=138 on the 390px screenshot, and
  the 3× crop agrees.

Each metric cell now draws its own window behind the label and value, so the
reading sits on its own history. It is decoration — `aria-hidden`, no pointer
events — and the number above it stays the measurement.

- **Window**: 20 samples at one every 30 s, i.e. ten minutes.
- **Scale**: the window's own range, with a floor of 4% of the metric's ceiling. A
  ceiling-based scale made every trace a flat line at its height (RAM moved 0.4% of
  128 GB); a pure auto-range turns noise into drama. The floor keeps a quiet window
  quiet without killing it.
- **Partial data is the normal case**, not an error, and it was tested as one: no
  samples draw nothing at all (the CPU cell with `series: []` stays blank behind its
  dash), one sample draws a level line, two draw a short segment. Long windows,
  short windows and the orderly case all use the same path.
- Traces take their metric's colour at 45% stroke over a 10% fill, and the text
  carries `z-index: 1` so it is never behind them.

## Round 33 — each reading is its own framed panel

The chart used to run cell to cell with a divider between columns. Each metric is
now a panel: `1px --hairline` frame, `--s3` radius, `rgba(0,0,0,.22)` well, and
`8px 4px` padding, laid out with an `--s2` gap instead of a divider.

- Sides stay at 4 rather than 8: the widest reading (`100/128 GB`, 71px of ink)
  leaves 26px of slack at 390 and 16px at 360 with 4, and only 2px at 390 with 8.
- The chart's margin from the frame is in its own geometry (`SIDE 8`, `TOP 14`,
  `FLOOR 88` in the viewBox), not in CSS offsets: an absolutely positioned SVG with
  a viewBox keeps its aspect ratio, so `inset` alone made it overflow the frame
  (measured `sparkBottomGap -44`).

## Round 34 — outline only, and shade under the text
