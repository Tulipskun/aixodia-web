# C4–C5 · Proposal A — Round the odd values

Swap the off-scale values for the nearest 4/8 step, keep the rest.

- 3→4, 6→8, 7→8, 10→12, 14→16, 15→16, 18→16 (or 20).
- Fonts: fold 17 into 16, 15 into 14, 10 into 11.

Effort: minutes. Risk: rounding 10 to 12 and 15 to 16 raises the card's height
and the desktop 24px scroll stays or grows.
