# Decision — type and space scale (C4–C5)

Chosen: **B (declare the scale, spend it)**, with C ruled out because the console
stylesheet carries the same drift and would couple a throwaway mock to 1,194 lines.

| lens | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| usability | 6 | 8 | 7 |
| visual craft | 6 | 9 | 8 |
| engineering | 9 | 9 | 3 |

Acceptance criteria: `layout-audit` `offScale` empty; ≤5 distinct font sizes;
`pageScrollY: 0` at all three viewports.
